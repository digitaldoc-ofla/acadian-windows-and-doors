Featured Wood

Sinker Cypress,
Louisiana's legend.

Over a century ago, old-growth bald cypress logs bound for the mill slipped beneath the rivers and bayous of Louisiana. Preserved in cool, oxygen-poor water, they waited, absorbing the minerals that give sinker cypress its rich, one-of-a-kind color.

Today these logs are carefully recovered and slowly dried. The result is a wood that is hard to come by and celebrated across Louisiana for its long life expectancy, strength and natural resistance to rot and insects, not to mention the beauty we all love.

“Want sinker cypress? We’ve got you covered.”
