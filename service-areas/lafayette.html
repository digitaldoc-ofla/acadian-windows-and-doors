Custom wood windows & doors in Lafayette & Acadiana

This is the heart of Acadiana, and the country our name comes from. The Acadian cottage, with its steep roof, deep front gallery and simple, sturdy openings, is the architecture of home here, and it still shapes the best new houses being built today.

From restorations near downtown to new homes in River Ranch, Youngsville and Broussard, we craft windows and doors with the proportions and character this region is known for.

Lafayette Architecture

Designed for Lafayette homes

Every part of Louisiana builds a little differently. These are the styles we see most in Lafayette, and how we design for each.

Acadian Cottages

Raised cottages with a full-width gallery call for paneled or half-glass doors, pairs of French doors onto the porch, and simple divided-lite windows.

Creole Plantation Style

Wide galleries with French doors on every bay and transoms above. Elegant openings designed to keep air moving through the house.

New Acadian & French Country

Today's custom homes borrow the old forms and add a statement: an arched entry, a round gable window or a sunburst over the door.

Climate & Craft

Built for South Louisiana

Heat, humidity and summer storms are a given in Acadiana. Wood and joinery have to be chosen with that in mind.

  • Sinker cypress recovered from Louisiana waters
  • Joinery glued and pinned for lasting strength
  • Finishes that can be refreshed rather than replaced

Our pick for Lafayette

Sinker Cypress

Bald cypress was cut across the Atchafalaya Basin and south Louisiana's swamps a century ago, and some of those logs never made it to the mill. Recovered sinker cypress is as local as wood gets.

Questions

Lafayette questions, answered

What makes a door or window "Acadian style"?

Simplicity and proportion: tall, narrow openings, paneled or half-glass doors, divided-lite windows and generous transoms. We can keep it traditional or add a refined touch like an arched top.

Can you build French doors for a porch or gallery wall?

Yes. Pairs of French doors along a gallery are a classic Acadiana look, and we size them to your porch and ceiling height.

Do you offer local wood?

Sinker cypress is recovered from Louisiana waters, so it's about as local as it gets. We also offer heart pine, mahogany, white oak, walnut and Spanish cedar.
