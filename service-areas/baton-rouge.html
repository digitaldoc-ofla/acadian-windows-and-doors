Custom wood windows & doors for Baton Rouge homes

Baton Rouge homes span two centuries, from cottages in Spanish Town and Beauregard Town to 1920s bungalows in the Garden District and new French Country estates off Highland Road. Each calls for something different at the front door.

We design and mill windows and doors around your home's style and your family's wants, in woods chosen to last through the Capital Region's heat and humidity.

Baton Rouge Architecture

Designed for Baton Rouge homes

Every part of Louisiana builds a little differently. These are the styles we see most in Baton Rouge, and how we design for each.

Historic Downtown Cottages

Spanish Town and Beauregard Town cottages and shotguns call for paneled doors, transoms and tall windows milled to their original proportions.

Garden District Bungalows & Revivals

Craftsman, Tudor and Colonial Revival homes from the 1920s and '30s, with divided-lite casements, arched entries and leaded glass accents.

New French Country & Acadian Homes

Grand arched entries, segment-top windows and round accent windows give new construction instant character and a sense of place.

Climate & Craft

Built for the Capital Region

Long, humid summers and heavy rains put wood to the test. We build for them from the first cut.

  • Heartwood species with natural rot resistance
  • Sills and drip edges shaped to shed water away from joints
  • Finishes chosen for strong south and west sun

Our pick for Baton Rouge

Heart Pine

Reclaimed longleaf heart pine echoes the floors and trim found in many older Baton Rouge homes, with a warm amber glow that deepens over time.

Questions

Baton Rouge questions, answered

Do you work on new construction as well as renovations?

Yes. We work with homeowners, builders and architects on new homes, and with homeowners on renovations and restorations. The earlier we're involved, the more design options you'll have.

How long do custom windows and doors take?

It depends on the size of the project and the wood you choose. You'll get a clear timeline along with your quote.

Can I see wood samples before choosing?

Yes. Samples are part of every design consultation, so you can see and feel each species before you decide.
