Designed for Shreveport–Bossier homes

Every part of Louisiana builds a little differently. These are the styles we see most in Shreveport–Bossier, and how we design for each.

Craftsman Bungalows

Highland's bungalows call for doors with simple upper lites, divided-upper windows and the ray-fleck figure of quarter-sawn oak.

Tudor & Revival Homes

Arched entries, leaded diamond glass and casement windows return these homes to their original charm.

Fairfield Avenue Classics

Grand entries with fanlights and sidelights, and tall windows sized for generous ceilings.

Climate & Craft

Built for North Louisiana

Summers here are hot and humid and winters bring real cold snaps, and that swing makes wood move. Construction has to allow for it.

  • Stable, quarter-sawn, properly dried lumber
  • Panels that float in their frames to allow seasonal movement
  • Weatherstripping chosen for both heat and cold

Our pick for Shreveport–Bossier

White Oak

Quarter-sawn white oak is the traditional Arts & Crafts wood, prized for its ray-fleck figure, strength and weather resistance.

Questions

Shreveport–Bossier questions, answered

Can you build Craftsman-style doors and windows?

Yes. Craftsman designs, with their clean lines, honest joinery and quarter-sawn oak, are a natural fit for custom millwork.

Will wood windows handle north Louisiana winters?

Yes. Well-built wood windows with good weatherstripping and quality glass perform well in heat and cold. We'll go over glass options during design.

Can I mix wood species in one project?

Yes. Some clients choose a showpiece wood for the front entry and a complementary species for the windows. We'll make sure the tones and finishes work together.
