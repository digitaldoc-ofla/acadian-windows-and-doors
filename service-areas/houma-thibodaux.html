Custom wood windows & doors in Houma, Thibodaux & Bayou Country

Along Bayou Lafourche and Bayou Terrebonne, homes are built with water in mind: raised high, with deep porches and openings meant to catch every breeze. Since Hurricane Ida in 2021, many families have been rebuilding and choosing windows and doors to last.

We craft custom windows and doors for historic downtown Thibodaux, raised homes and camps down the bayou, and new homes across Terrebonne and Lafourche parishes.

Questions

Houma & Thibodaux questions, answered

Can you rebuild windows and doors lost in a storm to match my home's original style?

Yes. From photos, salvaged pieces or surviving openings, we can mill replacements that bring back your home's original character.

Are wood doors a good choice for a raised coastal home?

Yes, with the right species, construction and finish. Durable heartwoods, careful joinery and regular finish care let wood doors thrive on the coast.

What maintenance do wood windows need near the coast?

Periodic inspection and a refresh of the exterior finish, more often on sunny or salt-exposed sides. We'll give you care guidance specific to your wood and finish.
