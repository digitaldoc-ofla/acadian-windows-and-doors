Custom wood windows & doors for New Orleans homes

Few cities ask more of a window or door than New Orleans. Tall French doors open onto iron-lace balconies, transoms crown nearly every entry, and the millwork has to survive heat, humidity and hurricane season while looking like it has always been there.

We mill windows and doors to match the profiles, proportions and woods of your home, whether you're restoring a Creole cottage in the Marigny or building new in Lakeview.

New Orleans Architecture

Designed for New Orleans homes

Every part of Louisiana builds a little differently. These are the styles we see most in New Orleans, and how we design for each.

Creole Townhouses & Cottages

Full-length French doors take the place of windows, opening onto balconies and banquettes. We build them tall, with true divided lites, to the exact height of your openings.

Shotguns & Camelbacks

On a narrow front, every opening counts: a paneled door under a transom and tall windows beside it. We match original moldings, transom bars and proportions.

Garden District Revivals

Greek Revival and Italianate homes call for grand entries: double doors, sidelights, fanlights and arched-top windows scaled to soaring ceilings.

Climate & Craft

Built for New Orleans weather

Moisture is the enemy of wood here, and Formosan termites are a well-known threat across the city. Wood choice and joinery matter as much as design.

  • Rot- and insect-resistant heartwoods like sinker cypress and Spanish cedar
  • Traditional mortise-and-tenon joints that hold tight through humidity swings
  • Finishes and sealing chosen for heat, sun and storm season

Our pick for New Orleans

Sinker Cypress

Cypress has been the wood of New Orleans millwork for generations. Sinker cypress gives you that same heritage, with the density of old-growth timber.

Questions

New Orleans questions, answered

Can you match the original millwork on my historic New Orleans home?

Yes. We measure your existing windows and doors, profile the moldings and muntins, and mill new pieces to match, so new work sits comfortably beside the old.

Can you build French doors to replace a window onto a balcony?

Converting a window to full-length French doors is a classic New Orleans upgrade. We'll size and build the doors to your opening and ceiling height.

What wood holds up best in New Orleans humidity?

Durable heartwoods such as sinker cypress, Spanish cedar and mahogany are excellent choices. We'll help you weigh appearance, budget and exposure for each opening.
