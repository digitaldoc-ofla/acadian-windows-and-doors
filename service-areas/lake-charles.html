Lake Charles Architecture

Designed for Lake Charles homes

Every part of Louisiana builds a little differently. These are the styles we see most in Lake Charles, and how we design for each.

Charpentier District Victorians

The district takes its name from the carpenters who built it (charpentier is French for carpenter). Its Queen Anne and Victorian homes feature paneled entries, transoms and decorative glass, and we mill to match.

Gulf Coast Raised Homes

Raised homes and coastal cottages need openings that handle wind-driven rain and full sun without giving up their beauty.

Lakefront Statement Homes

Big views deserve big openings: arched transoms, full-lite French doors and oval or round accent windows.

Climate & Craft

Built for the Gulf Coast

Questions

Lake Charles questions, answered

Can wood windows and doors work in a hurricane-prone area?

Yes, with the right wood, construction and glass. During design we'll talk through storm-protection and glass options for your home's exposure.

Can you rebuild windows and doors that were lost in a storm?

We can mill replacements to match what was lost, working from photos, salvaged pieces or measurements of surviving openings.

Can you match decorative glass in an older home?

We offer leaded and decorative glass options and will work toward a close match from photos or a surviving piece.
