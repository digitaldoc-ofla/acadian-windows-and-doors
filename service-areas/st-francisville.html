Custom wood windows & doors in St. Francisville & the Felicianas

With its antebellum plantation homes, historic downtown and rolling, oak-shaded hills, St. Francisville is one of Louisiana's most architecturally rich small towns. Homes here deserve millwork with the same sense of history.

We craft windows and doors for restorations, sympathetic additions and new homes in West and East Feliciana, built to look as though they've always belonged.

St. Francisville Architecture

Designed for St. Francisville homes

Every part of Louisiana builds a little differently. These are the styles we see most in St. Francisville, and how we design for each.

Plantation Homes

Federal and Greek Revival plantation homes feature tall multi-pane windows, paneled double doors and elegant fanlights.

Historic Downtown Cottages

Downtown's historic cottages and storefronts call for paneled doors, transoms and true divided-lite windows.

Country Estates

New homes in the hills call for character: arched entries, round or quatrefoil gable windows and doors milled from rare woods.

Climate & Craft

Built for the Felicianas

Shady, humid hillsides and long summers mean wood has to be chosen and finished to resist moisture.

  • Rot-resistant heartwoods
  • Lumber properly dried and acclimated before milling
  • Finishes matched to a historic appearance

Our pick for St. Francisville

Heart Pine

Heart pine was a common building wood in historic homes like these. Reclaimed heart pine lets new work match the old in color and character.

Questions

St. Francisville questions, answered

Can you make new doors for an addition that match my historic house?

Yes. We match profiles, proportions, wood species and finish so the addition reads as part of the original home.

Will new millwork look out of place on an old house?

Not when it's done right. Matching profiles, proportions and wood lets new windows and doors sit naturally beside original work.
