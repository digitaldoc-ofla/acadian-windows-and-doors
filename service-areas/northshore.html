The Northshore · St. Tammany Parish

Custom wood windows & doors on the Northshore

From Old Mandeville cottages near the lakefront to estate homes in Covington, Madisonville and Folsom, the Northshore is home to some of Louisiana's most beautiful custom houses. The front door is where that story starts.

We design and mill windows and doors for Northshore homeowners, builders and architects, in rare woods and in the styles that suit St. Tammany's piney woods and waterfront living.

Old Mandeville & Covington Cottages

Raised cottages with deep porches: half-glass doors, tall windows and transoms that let the breeze through.

Northshore Estates

Lakefront & Riverfront Homes

Questions

Covington & Mandeville questions, answered

Do you work with builders and architects?

Yes. We're glad to work from architectural drawings, coordinate with your builder and provide shop drawings for approval.

Can you build a matching set of windows and doors for a whole house?

Yes. With a whole-home package, every profile, wood tone and finish matches, from the front door to the back porch.

Can I get a round or arched window for my gable?

Absolutely. Oculus, oval, eyebrow, gothic and sunburst windows can all be built to fit your gable.
